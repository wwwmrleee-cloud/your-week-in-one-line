# Candidate lines — Your Week in One Line

Eight candidate one-liners were compared head-to-head against the judging
criteria (in order): ① one line summing up the week (win / lose / chaos);
② a real laugh or a sharp turn; ③ one concrete detail; ④ every word earns
its place; ⑤ fresh, not a template.

## Winner (90 chars)

> Monday I planned to rest; by Friday the fridge had a personality and I owed it an apology.

Why it won:

- Monday → Friday arc covers the whole week in one sentence.
- The sharp turn lands in the last third ("I owed it an apology").
- The fridge is a real detail from this week — passive-aggressive fridge
  notes and a 3 a.m. fridge text both happened.
- 90 characters, no filler words.

## The other seven

All eight were scored on the same five criteria; the winner beat each of
them in pairwise comparison on the arc + turn + concrete-detail combination.
The losing drafts are not kept here — only the winning line ships.
